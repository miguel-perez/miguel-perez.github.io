Summary

Product designer and design researcher, building software since 2008. Started as a freelance pixel artist and self-taught front-end developer, led front-end for enterprise e-commerce brands, moved into research and product design, founded and ran a Design Center of Excellence at a healthcare data company, ran an independent research and design practice with staffed subcontractors, and most recently served as a Digital Service Expert with Colorado Digital Service on the State's payroll modernization. Since 2020, building and evaluating LLM tooling for qualitative research, benchmarked against expert human coding. The pattern across roles: build the measurement before the redesign, then ship the thing and the system that maintains it.
