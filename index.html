How the work runs

The part most projects skip comes first.

  1. 01
    Measure first. The hypothesis, the number that would change our minds, then the instrument.
  2. 02
    Design from the evidence. Personas from factor analysis, journeys from interviews, concepts as testable hypotheses.
  3. 03
    Build it, or build alongside. Front-end in your toolchain, and the spec I'd want to build myself.
  4. 04
    Leave the system. The repository, the protocol, the design system, the training. Your team runs it after I'm gone.
  5. 05
    Put a clock on it. Every rule expires on a schedule unless it earns its place again.

The two arrows that touch people are crossings, so they come with consent, a meter, and a way to say no.
